## Research and Labs

### NGIS Lab · Next-Generation Information Security

My interests at NGIS Lab include Active Directory security, Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) and lateral movement. My reading notes connect attack behavior with the evidence and context needed to understand it.

[Explore NGIS Lab](../security/#ngis)

### MIRAGE Lab · Agentic Red Team Operations

At MIRAGE Lab, I explore network attack and defense, digital forensics and agent-assisted security research. My research project **h1d3&C**, currently in preparation, focuses on autonomous LLM agents and host-side anti-forensic behavior, evaluated through the evidence that remains available for forensic reconstruction.

[Explore MIRAGE Lab and research projects](../security/#mirage)
